Water, agriculture and environmental observations

Water monitoring, agricultural imagery and snow observations in Kazakhstan: three datasets to explore for regional research.

  • Water Dynamics and Environmental Monitoring Dataset

    Astana IT University · cc-by

  • AgroTwin: A Pixel-Level Agricultural Dataset for Crop Monitoring, Anomaly Detection, and Machine Learning (2020–2025)

    Astana IT University · cc-by

  • GEOREFERENCED SNOW DEPTH AND SNOW WATER EQUIVALENT DATASET (2025) FROM EAST KAZAKHSTAN REGION

    Sarsen Amanzholov East Kazakhstan State University · cc-by

Compare the observation period, geography, units and collection method before combining these sources. The datasets have not been merged into a common time series.
