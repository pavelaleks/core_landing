For universities

Your university's research data can earn money

Companies building artificial intelligence are looking for knowledge about Central Asia: research results and observations, expert texts, speech in local languages. Material like this has sat in department archives for years. We help the university prepare it and sell the right to use it — the data stays the university's property, and most of the revenue goes to the university and its authors.

Step by step: from memorandum to first payout

  1. 1MoUIntent, confidentiality, pilot collection. No sales obligations.
  2. 2Order & accessRector's order appoints the steward and signatory — portal opens.
  3. 3Revenue-share agreementThe university lets CORE sell access to the data and receives its share. Signed once.
  4. 4Rights and personal-data checkWe check who owns the rights and whether authors and study participants consented, and remove personal data.
  5. 5A sheet for each datasetWhat is in it, how it may and may not be used, the price. Signed by the university's responsible person.
  6. 6Publish & payoutsQuarterly reports and payouts to the university's extra-budgetary account.

Model terms for universities

  • Up to 70% of net revenue to the university when a dataset is ready to license as is
  • University receives 40–50% when CORE digitizes, cleans and annotates a collection at its own cost
  • Recommended author pool: at least 30% of the university's share (via internal regulation)
  • CORE funds preparation, annotation and sales — no cost to the university
  • The right to rule out certain kinds of buyers and uses
  • Right to stop new licenses for any dataset at any time
  • Public attribution of the university and authors on every dataset

Indicative terms; final terms are set in the agreement in line with the university charter and ministry requirements.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Why only universities as suppliers, not individual researchers?

Rights to data created in the course of employment generally belong to the university, and human-subjects research needs ethics approval. Working through authorized university staff gives buyers a clean rights chain and gives researchers protection and a revenue share. Researchers take part through their university's portal.

What does it cost a university to join?

Nothing. CORE funds preparation, annotation, hosting and sales. That is why the university's share is lower — 40–50% instead of 70% — for collections CORE digitizes and annotates at its own cost.

Can we withdraw a dataset?

Yes. You can stop new licenses for a dataset at any time. Licenses already granted run until the end of their term.

How do you handle personal data?

We anonymize before delivery, store data in line with Uzbekistan's Personal Data Law, and identifying data never enters the public catalog. Re-identification is contractually prohibited.
