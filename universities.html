How CORE works

Create a dataset description explaining what the data contains, where it came from and which tasks it can help with. Add links to files, publications and documentation as needed.

First save a draft in your account. Once you have selected a license and your account is verified, you can publish the description in the catalog.

The CORE agents and services page presents AI use cases, proposed connections and examples of working with data.

What to prepare for publication

Start with one dataset that could help other researchers or companies. It may contain tables, text corpora, images, observations or survey results.

  • A title and description are enough for your first draft.
  • As you prepare the dataset, add its source, material links, formats, languages, size and version.
  • Before publishing, make sure you have the right to share the materials. Review the description and linked files for personal and confidential information.

Who can see your data

You can access a draft in your account. Once published, its description becomes visible to other visitors. Only the owner can edit the description.

The terms of use and links you added are published with the description. Access to the linked files is managed in the storage where they are hosted.

If you select Metadata only or Access by agreement, remove links of the Data files type before publishing. Read the license text for the full terms.

How to get started

  1. Create an account

    Register with your email and password. Complete your profile after signing in.

  2. Choose your university and role

    Choose your university in the profile form and select your role in the organization.

  3. Add your first dataset

    Open My datasets, choose Add dataset, enter a title and description, then save the draft.

  4. Check your account status

    Your account shows its verification status. Publishing becomes available after CORE verifies your account.

Publishing and updates

How to publish a dataset

Open Publication terms in the dataset editor, select a license and read its full text. Save the description, then open its page and choose Publish.

How to edit the description

Open your dataset in My datasets and choose Edit. Saved changes update the published description and links.

How to unpublish a dataset

Open your dataset and choose Unpublish. You can then find it in My datasets, edit it and publish it again.
