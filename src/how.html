The data path: from a university archive to an API

Five stages and three ways data reaches CORE. Pick a way and your role — and see what happens at each stage and what it means for you.

Closed by default

Nothing from a university's data is published without the university's decision. The catalog shows only the datasheet.

Rights and personal data — before any sale

We check authors' rights and remove personal details during preparation. Data is stored under the country's law.

Data stays alive

New versions, a change log, the same API address. The university sees who bought its data and why.
