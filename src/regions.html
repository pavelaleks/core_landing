How the data joins: the Syrdarya example

Gulistan State University's insurance survey (353 people) sits on the same region grid as the official demographics. So answers can be compared with the region's population structure — and the sample for a new survey can be built from quotas in this very table.


  






  

    

      For your task
      
What open sources don't have, we collect

    

    

      
A survey through the region's universities
Consumer and financial behaviour, digital services, trust, intentions. Sociology and economics departments run the survey in the regions you need; we build the sample from official sex, age and urban–rural quotas; the study goes through the university's ethics board.

      
Joining sources
Your data, government statistics (e.g. 690 CC BY datasets on Kyrgyzstan's portal) and university data — in one region code, one format and three languages, across four countries.

      
Countries and contract
We work with companies from Central Asia, Russia and elsewhere. Contract in Russian or English, settlement in USD; currency and jurisdiction by agreement. Personal data stays in the country: you receive aggregates and de-identified answers.
