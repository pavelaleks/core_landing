Who can use the data

  • Education companies and training teams — research results for tests and learning exercises.
  • Language model developers — prepared research text corpora for training and evaluating models.
  • AI developers — annotated images for training and testing recognition models.
  • Companies — up-to-date survey data to understand their audience.
  • Researchers — comparable data from earlier research to study changes and test new hypotheses.

What collaboration looks like

  1. The university describes its available datasets and repositories.
  2. The partner sets out a task and the amount of data needed.
  3. The research team checks whether the content, volume and freshness of the data fit the task.
  4. If the data fits, the parties agree on a subscription to a data selection or a service based on it.
