Frequently asked questions

Who can add data?

Representatives of universities and companies can add data. After registering, select your organization in your profile and create a dataset draft. You can publish once the CORE team verifies your account.

Registration and getting started
What materials can I add?

Tables, text corpora, images, observations and survey results. A title and description are enough to save a dataset draft.

Preparing your data
What becomes public when I publish?

The dataset description, selected license and links you added. If the files are hosted elsewhere, their access settings are managed in that storage.

Who can see the data
How do I choose a license?

Open Publication terms in the dataset editor, select a license and read its full text. The options are Metadata only, Access by agreement and the open CC BY 4.0 license.

Compare license terms
How do I access the data?

The dataset description explains its license and how to obtain the data. Follow the material links or contact the owner if access needs to be agreed.

Browse the catalog
Can I update or unpublish a dataset?

Yes. In My datasets, you can edit the description and links or unpublish the dataset. You can still access an unpublished dataset in your account.
