How it works

Three steps — from a department archive to university revenue

  1. 1

    The university shares data

    Archives, text collections, speech recordings, field and lab observations. The university decides what can be offered and what cannot.

  2. 2

    We prepare it

    We clean and digitize it, mark up what matters together with the university's experts, check authors' rights and remove personal details.

  3. 3

    Companies pay — the university earns

    The university stays the owner; the buyer gets the files and the right to use them under a contract. The university keeps 70% of revenue if the data is ready as is, or 40–50% if we digitize and mark it up at our own cost. Part of the university's share goes to the authors.

Example

One dataset — useful for AI and for business

De-identified records of 2,313 COVID-19 patients from the Kazakh National Medical University.

Before

A table where microbes are free text, partly in Russian (“нет роста”, “отрицательно”), antibiotic susceptibility is written six different ways, and diagnoses have no codes.

What we did

Mapped to medical standards — ICD-10, LOINC, ATC, NCBI taxonomy. Checked ranges and logic. Described each of the 59 fields in three languages. Kept the authors and licence.

Who uses it

Medical AI trains and tests a model on patients from the region. Pharma and insurers measure outcomes: 5.1% mortality in the Delta wave versus 1.2–1.8% before. The university receives a share of revenue, with authors credited.

Questions

Frequently asked

What can I buy today?

Regional data and registries (universities, clinics), surveys through universities, analytics, a pilot, custom data and a pre-order of the model check. Ready datasets from university archives will appear as agreements with universities are signed.

How are you different from open databases online?

Open repositories show what is already published, as is: we checked 548 such records by the region's scholars and 143 hold no data at all. We bring data to standards, check rights and personal details — and work with what is not online: university archives.

How do you handle personal data?

We remove personal details before anything reaches a buyer. University data will be stored as Uzbekistan's Personal Data Law requires — on servers in Uzbekistan or where the law allows. Buyers may not try to identify people from the data.

Can a foreign company buy data?

Yes — companies from Central Asia, Russia and elsewhere. Contract in Russian or English, settlement in USD; currency and jurisdiction by agreement. Personal data stays in the country: the buyer receives aggregated and de-identified data.
