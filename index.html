The gap

80M people. Five languages. Almost no data you can legally train on.

Global models are weak on Central Asia — low-resource languages, regional history, law and realia. The region's best data is locked inside university archives: unlabelled, undocumented and legally ambiguous.

01

Web scraping is a dead end

The open web in Uzbek or Tajik is thin and noisy — and scraped data is now a legal liability for any serious AI company.

02

University archives are invisible

Decades of observations, corpora, expeditions and expert knowledge sit in universities — too small to reach AI labs alone, too complex to license without help.

03

No trust, no deal

Buyers need provenance, anonymization and documentation. Without them, no legal team signs off on a license.

The legal path: from MoU to first payout

  1. 1MoUIntent, confidentiality, pilot collection. No sales obligations.
  2. 2Order & accessRector's order appoints the steward and signatory — portal opens.
  3. 3Framework agreementNon-exclusive distribution license to CORE + revenue share. Signed once.
  4. 4Rights & PII auditWorks-for-hire, author consents, third-party content; anonymization under the Law of Uzbekistan on Personal Data.
  5. 5Dataset scheduleDatasheet, permitted uses, exclusions and pricing — signed in the portal.
  6. 6Publish & payoutsQuarterly reports and payouts to the university's extra-budgetary account.

Model terms for universities

  • University receives 70% of net license revenue on datasets from its collections
  • Recommended author pool: at least 30% of the university's share (via internal regulation)
  • CORE funds preparation, annotation and sales — no cost to the university
  • Veto over buyer categories and use cases
  • Right to stop new licenses for any dataset at any time
  • Public attribution of the university and authors on every dataset

Indicative terms; final terms are set in the agreement in line with the university charter and ministry requirements.

Trust & compliance

Provenance is the product

The market's lesson: scandals over selling student data, authors suing publishers. So in CORE, every record traces back to its source and legal basis.

Rights chain

The legal basis — contract, consent, open license — is recorded per dataset; without it, our catalog validator rejects the dataset.

Personal data

Anonymized before release. Storage follows Art. 27¹ of Uzbekistan's Personal Data Law — sensitive categories stay on servers in Uzbekistan.

Datasheets

Every dataset ships with a Datasheets-for-Datasets passport, a Frictionless Data Package schema and field-completeness metrics.

Open stays open

We never enclose CC0 or ODbL data. We charge for verification, enrichment, normalization and refresh.

Acceptable use

Re-identification, surveillance and resale of raw data are prohibited. Universities can exclude buyer categories.

Reproducibility

Every dataset rebuilds from versioned code with checksums. Buyers know exactly what they received.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Why only universities as suppliers, not individual researchers?

Rights to data created in the course of employment generally belong to the university, and human-subjects research needs ethics approval. Working through authorized university staff gives buyers a clean rights chain and gives researchers protection and a revenue share. Researchers take part through their university's portal.

How are you different from Hugging Face or open data portals?

Open platforms host what's already published. We work with what isn't online: university collections, expert annotation and rights verification. We publish samples and our benchmark on Hugging Face ourselves.

What does a “non-exclusive term license” mean?

The buyer may use the dataset for agreed purposes (training, evaluation, RAG) during the term, while the university keeps ownership and can license to others. Exclusivity windows are available for a premium with the university's consent.

How do you handle personal data?

We anonymize before delivery, store data in line with Uzbekistan's Personal Data Law, and identifying data never enters the public catalog. Re-identification is contractually prohibited.

Can a foreign company license data?

Yes. We contract and invoice in USD; cross-border service exports are handled under Uzbekistan's currency regulations.

What does it cost a university to join?

Nothing. CORE funds preparation, annotation, hosting and sales from its share of revenue.
