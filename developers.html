OpenAlex W7203955937UNI-UZ-0138 · Samarkand State University2026CC-BY

Supporting data for “New records and habitat modeling of Limnatis paluda (Praobdellidae) in southern Uzbekistan”

This dataset contains supporting data for a study of Limnatis paluda in Uzbekistan, including occurrence records, specimen and habitat photographs, COI sequence and alignment data, haplotype-network files, and MaxEnt habitat-suitability modeling outputs. The study documents new records from southern Uzbekistan and evaluates current and projected habitat suitability under the SSP3-7.0 climate scenario.
